Daily strategy & scoring guide

Alliance Duel

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

Event overview
Purpose

Compete for points against an opposing alliance to determine the winner.

Goal

Win the daily point battle at reset. The alliance with the higher total wins the day.

Rewards

Unlock daily reward chests as you cross personal point thresholds — worth chasing even on a day you're losing.

Golden rule

Save your resources for the right day. Every phase rewards a different resource — hoarding is the whole strategy.

Day 1

Enhance Raven

Focus: Raven + Herb Gathering

Raven & Falcon

＋Save Falcon Quests from the weekend and claim them Monday.
＋Use saved Raven Essence and Raven Fruit.
＋Run rallies if Raven Survival Battle is active.

Herb gathering (Lv. 8–10)

＋Gather Lv. 8–10 Herb Tiles, or the highest available.
＋Gathering takes about 6 hours — start early so marches finish during the phase.
＋Send all squads, and immediately resend them to keep earning points.

Build prep

＋Start your longest building upgrades.
＋Do not collect completed buildings — save them for Tuesday.
Tips
＋Keep all squads gathering.
＋Stop using Raven items once you reach the point cap.
＋Save Falcon Quests Sunday night.
Day 2

Build Territory

Focus: Building Speedups

Build every day

＋Keep builders active with a long upgrade always running.

Save speedups

＋Never use Building Speedups before Tuesday.
＋Save all Building Speedups for Building Day.

Building day

＋Finish Monday's long upgrades using saved Speedups.
＋Start another long upgrade right after finishing.
Tips
＋Hoard Building Speedups.
＋Plan long builds to finish Tuesday.
＋Save completed buildings from Monday before collecting — preparation makes Tuesday one of the easiest max-point days.
Day 3

Tech Research

Focus: Research Speedups + Research Scrolls

Research every day

＋Always keep research running — prioritize long projects.

Save research speedups

＋Never use Research Speedups on normal days.
＋Save all Speedups for Wednesday.

Research scrolls

＋Never use Scrolls early — save every one for Tech Research Day.
＋Scrolls give maximum Alliance Duel points.
Tips
＋Finish long research Wednesday, then start another before logging off.
＋Hoarding Speedups and Scrolls makes Wednesday an easy max-point day.
Day 4

Enhance Heroes

Focus: Hero Tickets

Hoard hero tickets

＋Save Hero Recruitment Tickets all week — open them Thursday.

Use in bulk

＋Use tickets only if you have 300+ — large summons give better RNG and results.

If you have less

＋Avoid small summons — focus on hero and skill upgrades instead.
Tips
＋Save all Hero Tickets — large summons earn more Alliance Duel points.
＋Bulk summons improve chances for higher-quality heroes.
＋Under 300 tickets? Keep saving.
Day 5

Mixed Day

Focus: Build · Research · Train

Objective

＋Earn points from Building, Research, and Troop Training — using all three gives the highest score.

Falcon quest

＋Complete one Falcon Quest for quick points.

Construction & research

＋Start or finish a building — Building Speedups count.
＋Start or complete research — Research Speedups count.

Troop training

＋Train troops — Training Boosts and Speedups count.

Minimum speedups

＋1-minute Building Speedup
＋1-minute Research Speedup
＋1-minute Training Speedup
Best method
＋Start a building, start research, and train troops — then use Speedups on all three.
＋Splitting points across all tasks helps your alliance reach higher chests faster.
Pro tips
＋Watch the scoreboard — if winning big near reset, stop spending and save for tomorrow.
＋Reset snipe — be online 15 minutes before reset to finish with Speedups.
＋Unlock chests — aim for personal point thresholds for Gems and Speedups, even on a losing day.

Save smart. Spend on time. Win the day.
