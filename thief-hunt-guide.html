Event guide

Thief Hunt

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The Story

Amidst the apocalypse, the Royal City fell, and a group of thieves stole the precious Lion Coins.

Enemy Introduction
Gold Thieves

Small thieves hiding with stolen Lion Coins. Chase and capture them!

Thief Leader

A massive thief leader who commands the Gold Thieves — far stronger than ordinary enemies.

Defeat 4 to 8 Gold Thieves to spawn the Thief Leader.

Event Instructions
1

Once Thief Hunt begins, hidden Gold Thieves are exposed to everyone. Chase the nearest Gold Thief through the event interface.

2

Capturing Gold Thieves may occasionally spawn the Thief Leader. It's far stronger than ordinary enemies, so rally with others to defeat it — the boss grants generous rewards.

3

Capturing Gold Thieves lets you retrieve Lion Coins, which can be exchanged for various rewards in the corresponding shop.

4

Players are ranked by total damage dealt to the Thief Leader. Higher damage and ranking mean richer rewards, sent to your mailbox after the event ends.

5

After the event, all participating players receive participation rewards.

Tips
＋Focus on nearby thieves to capture them quickly.
＋Work with allies to defeat the Thief Leader.
＋Deal more damage to climb the ranks and earn better rewards.
