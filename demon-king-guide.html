Maximizing Damage

1. Offense Over Defense
+The Demon King doesn't fight back — no tanks needed.
+Use 4 DPS + 1 Support, or 3 DPS + 2 Supports.
+Supports boost your damage dealers — choose heroes whose skills complement each other.
+Include a Purple SSR damage dealer for monster damage bonuses.

If a Tank has more skill levels than your other damage dealers, it may still be the better choice — until a DPS catches up on skills.

2. Gear Priorities
Sword

Use the highest-level Gold UR Sword you can get. Boosts base ATK and Crit Chance — a +0 UR sword outperforms a +30 SSR sword.

Gloves

Use Level 30 SSR Gloves. At level 30 they add +12.25% damage to monsters, applied after all other calculations, while UR gloves only add ATK + Damage Resistance.

Boots & Chest

Not important against the Demon King — they give DEF, Damage Resistance, and HP, which you don't need here. Use whatever level you have.

3. Skills & Cooldowns
+More stars mean higher skill ranks — your biggest source of damage.
+Read every skill and build synergies — don't just chase the highest ATK.
+Stacking buffs multiplies your damage.
+Skills trigger in order of their cooldowns — a slower buff skill can miss the window to boost a faster DPS skill.
+8-star UR heroes get Tenacity (min. Lv. 30): +ATK, HP, DEF and reduced cooldown speed.
4. Faction Bonus

Factions work like rock-paper-scissors: Warrior > Warlock > Ranger > Warrior. Using the opposing faction deals 50% more damage.

Power > Faction when the investment gap is big.
A much more invested "wrong" faction can still outperform the advantaged one.

5. Party Placement
+No tanks needed, so placement is about maximizing damage, not survival.
+There's no perfect answer — placement affects damage in mysterious ways.
+Small changes can lead to huge damage differences.

Experiment across your attacks each day, track your results, and keep what works.
