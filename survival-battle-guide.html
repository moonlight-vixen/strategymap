Daily event guide

Survival Battle

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The event

A daily event where you compete against players of a similar Sanctuary level by completing themed objectives. Each day, 12–20 similarly-leveled players are randomly matched into the same competition group.

Event schedule
6
Rounds per day
4h
Per round

Each round has its own theme, with its own set of quests.

Stage rewards

As you accumulate points during a theme, you unlock 4 Stage Rewards based on your score. These are claimed separately for each of the six daily themes.

Survival medal milestones

Survival Medals contribute toward special daily milestone rewards, claimed once per day.

Strategy

Collect Stage Rewards across multiple themes — that's the key to earning enough Survival Medals to unlock all three milestone rewards each day.

Stack every theme. Claim every medal.
