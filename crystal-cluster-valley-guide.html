First-time player guide

Crystal Cluster Valley

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The event

A 30-minute PvP gathering event. Gather crystals, kill Plague Rats for XP, fight smart, and earn the most Crystal Points to claim victory.

Troop deaths are temporary

Troops only die once the event ends.

Healing required

Use healing speedups to heal wounded troops quickly.

Shields don't work

Everyone can attack everyone.

Highest crystals wins

Collect, fight, and dominate the leaderboard.

Event timeline
0:00 – 10:00

Early game: level up fast

＋Kill Plague Rats to gain XP and unlock Event Skills.
＋Gather nearby Crystal Nodes.
＋Don't chase PvP early.
＋Unlocking your skills is the top priority.
10:00 – 23:00

Mid game: farm & rotate

＋Focus on Level 2 and Level 3 Crystal Clusters.
＋Rotate between nodes — never sit idle.
＋Grab Instant Crystal spawns whenever they appear.
＋Pick your fights — only fight if you can win.
23:00 – 30:00

Final push: go big

＋Strong players should contest the Throne.
＋Smaller accounts should keep farming nearby Crystal Nodes.
＋This is where the highest points are earned.
Crystal node priority
Level 1
Easy to gather. Low crystals. Best for early game only.
Level 2
Best balance of reward and safety. Less contested.
Level 3
Highest reward. Heavily contested — be ready to fight.

Instant Crystal Drops spawn randomly and grant huge rewards. Always go get them!

Skill tree — recommended order
1

Crystal Income — increase crystals gained over time.

2

March Speed — move faster, gather more, escape easier.

3

Crystal Capture Bonus — more crystals for every node you capture.

4

Gathering Speed — collect crystals faster at all clusters.

5

PvP Skill — choose based on your playstyle.

Battle tips
＋Fight only battles you can win quickly.
＋Scout players before attacking.
＋Avoid fighting stronger opponents.
＋Keep every march moving at all times.
Do & don't
Do
  • ✓Kill rats early to unlock skills
  • ✓Keep all marches busy
  • ✓Gather higher-level crystals as you grow
  • ✓Grab instant crystal spawns
  • ✓Scout before attacking
  • ✓Heal wounded troops quickly
Don't
  • ✕Fight immediately
  • ✕Attack players much stronger than you
  • ✕Stay on low-level crystals too long
  • ✕Chase revenge
  • ✕Forget instant crystal spawns
  • ✕Let marches sit idle
Remember

Crystal gathering earns more points than unnecessary PvP. Smart farming and constant march activity are the keys to high rankings.

Farm smart. Fight smarter. Win together.
