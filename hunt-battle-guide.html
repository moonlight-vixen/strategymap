Alliance PvE event guide

Hunt Battle

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The event

A cooperative Alliance PvE event where members work together to defeat 15 waves of Blights (infected enemies). Teamwork and coordination are the keys to clearing higher difficulties and earning better rewards.

Event overview
20+
Minimum participants
15
Waves of enemies
＋Started by R4/R5.
＋5-minute preparation phase.
＋March time is fixed at 15 seconds for all players.
Objective
＋Defeat all 15 waves.
＋Clear the highest difficulty your alliance can handle.
＋Work together to maximize alliance kills and rewards.
How it works
1

R4/R5 choose the difficulty before the event starts.

2

Members prepare during the 5-minute setup phase.

3

Once the battle begins, all players work together to defeat each wave.

4

Every wave becomes more difficult than the last.

Strategy
＋Choose a difficulty your alliance can consistently clear.
＋Have as many members online as possible.
＋Focus attacks on the same targets.
＋Coordinate through alliance chat.
＋Save buffs and stronger marches for the later waves.

Hero Upgrade Materials
