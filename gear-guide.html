Gear Guide

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The one rule

Gear stats build power — rarity is only a ceiling.

A maxed lower-rarity piece beats a fresh higher-rarity piece at level 0. Don't reset your power chasing a shinier color.

N  →  R  →  SR  →  SSR  →  UR

Match slot to role
RolePriority slotsWhy
DPSWeapon → GauntletsScales attack output
TankArmor → BootsScales survivability
SupportBoots → ArmorKeeps them alive to buff/heal

Never cross the streams — offense stats on a tank, or defense stats on a DPS, waste materials.

Upgrade order
1

Get all crafted gear to a baseline level before specializing.

2

Push your main DPS — weapon, then gauntlets.

3

Push your main Tank — armor, then boots.

4

Only then invest in support and secondary heroes.

One hero fully geared beats three heroes half-geared.

SSR → UR, the right way
The mistake

Switching to fresh UR gear at +0 lowers your power immediately.

The rule

Only switch once the upgraded UR piece equals or beats your SSR piece.

Keep your leveled SSR set as main gear. Upgrade UR in the background, slot by slot, and swap each one only once it actually passes the old piece.

Switch order: Weapon → Gauntlets → Armor → Boots

What not to do
✕Full-set swap at once — go piece by piece.
✕Splitting materials evenly across every hero.
✕Downgrading your main hero's power to chase rarity.
✕Ignoring role matching on gear slots.
Scale into it — don't reset your power
