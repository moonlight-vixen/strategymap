Diamond Guide

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

Overview

Diamonds fund speedups, heroes, growth, and more. Free methods stack up faster than you'd think if you're consistent about them.

Part 1 · How to get diamonds

Free methods
1. Daily Tasks

Most consistent

＋Always complete all daily missions.
＋Those small diamond rewards stack quickly.
＋Treat it as a non-negotiable daily habit.
2. Events

Biggest payouts

＋Focus on Alliance Duel phases, Hunt Battle, Elixir Scramble, and Kingdom War.
＋Even partial participation earns diamonds.
3. Achievement / Progress
＋Check Growth tabs, milestone rewards, and hidden achievements.
＋People forget these and leave diamonds sitting there.
4. Alliance Gifts
＋Stay active when teammates purchase packs.
＋Claim all gifts — passive diamonds over time.
5. Map Exploration
＋Click random things on the map and do PvE content.
＋Small amounts, but consistent — every little bit counts.
6. Mail & System Rewards
＋Check mail constantly — dev gifts, compensation, and event rewards hide there.
＋Don't let free diamonds expire.

Part 2 · Don't waste your diamonds

Best uses — high value, big impact
Shields

Your #1 priority. Protect your resources, hospital, and progress. Always keep backup shields ready.

Event Stamina

Use during big events (Alliance Duel, Hero Events, etc.). More stamina means more points and better rewards.

Important Event Participation

Spend to unlock, rank up, or complete event milestones for high-value rewards. Timing is everything.

Strategic Teleports

Use for territory, dungeons, boss moves, or during Kingdom War. Smart moves win wars.

VIP progression

Early VIP levels give excellent daily bonuses. Small steps daily mean huge long-term value.

High-Value Limited Events

Look for limited-time offers, discount packs, and event bundles with the best diamond value.

Emergency War Mobility

Save diamonds for emergencies during war — relocating, reinforcing, countering threats.

Only sometimes — situational, be careful
Speedups

Okay to use if you're close to a big event or need a boost.

Don't speed everything. Be selective.

Shop Refreshes

Can be worth it during events or if you're hunting for key items.

Set a limit. Don't refresh endlessly.

Extra March Slots

Helpful if you're very active and need more marches for gathering or war.

Get as needed — not a priority for everyone.

Gear Materials

Good if you're actively working on hero/gear upgrades.

Buy only what you truly need.

Late VIP Levels

Early VIP levels are great value. Higher levels become very expensive for smaller gains.

Worth it only if you're a heavy spender.

Biggest wastes — don't do it
Random Spins

Low chance, high regret. If it's not a target event, don't spin "just because." Commit or skip.

Speeding Everything

Instant speeds feel good now, but they slow your growth long-term. Patience builds power.

Low-Value Shop Items

Don't buy basic resources, common items, or random materials with diamonds. Terrible value.

Reviving Bad Attacks

Don't bring back a losing fight. It's almost always a waste of diamonds.

Panic Spending During War

Reacting emotionally leads to poor decisions. Stay calm. Plan ahead.

Cosmetic-Only Purchases (Early Game)

Looks are fun, but power is what wins wars. Save cosmetics for later.

The golden rule: if it doesn't help your growth, war, or long-term power — don't spend it.

Small actions today. Massive power tomorrow.
