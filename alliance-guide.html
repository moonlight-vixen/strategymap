Alliance Guide

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

Why join

One of the most important steps you can take.

An Alliance provides valuable bonuses and unlocks Alliance-exclusive events and features that can't be accessed alone.

Two core systems
Alliance Gifts

Rewards the whole Alliance when members contribute.

Alliance Research

Collective buffs researched together as a group.

Alliance gifts
1

When a member buys a pack containing an Alliance Chest, every member of the Alliance receives an Alliance Gift.

2

Winning rallies against Elite Blight and Wandering Blight also grants gifts.

There are 20 Alliance Gift levels — each one unlocks new bonuses and features. Higher-quality chests earn more level progress.

Level progress grows every time members earn and claim Alliance Chests.

Alliance research

Works like personal Technology Research, but is completed collectively by the entire Alliance. Some technologies unlock special functions — like auto-joining Alliance rallies while you're offline.

Development

Improves growth and economy.

  • Construction speed
  • Research speed
  • Gathering speed
  • Resource production
  • Other growth buffs
War

Enhances military capability.

  • Increased troop load
  • Faster troop training
  • Rally speed
  • Combat buffs
