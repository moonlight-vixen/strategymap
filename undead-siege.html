Alliance Event Guide

Undead Siege

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The threat

The Death Prison, shrouded in mist, has fallen to plague. Inmates and guards have become savage undead, ready to assault your territories, Doctors.

How it starts
1

Alliance members complete Falcon Quests to gather clues and help locate the Death Prison.

2

Once enough clues are gathered, R5/R4 leaders dispatch falcons to search for the prison.

3

Leaders choose a difficulty based on the Alliance's strength.

4

Confirm timing with members and schedule the final challenge.

Defense
Shields don't work

Shields cannot withstand undead attacks.

Defend the walls

Deploy defensive troops on your Walls.

Rewards

Personal and Alliance rewards are distributed based on successful defense waves. The more waves you defend, the greater your rewards.

R5 / R4 checklist
＋Coordinate members to complete Falcon Quests for clues.
＋Stockpile resources ahead of the siege.
＋Confirm battle times with the Alliance.
＋Choose difficulty based on Alliance strength.
＋Lead the Alliance to face the Undead Siege.

Man the walls. Defend every wave.

Top 7 Mistakes to Avoid
Why This Matters

Undead Siege is one of the most teamwork-focused events in the game. Success isn't just about power — it depends on preparation, coordination, and avoiding these seven common mistakes.

1
Forgetting to Station Heroes

Even with strong troops, your defense is far weaker without heroes on the wall.

Fix: Before the event begins, double-check your strongest heroes and best squad are assigned to your wall.

2
Ignoring Wall Defense

Your city won't defend itself automatically — the wall is your primary defense.

Fix: Place your strongest squad on the wall and make sure it's fully prepared before the first wave starts.

3
Starting Without Preparation

The event can't begin until your alliance fills the Clue progress bar to 100%.

Fix: Collect clues ahead of time through Falcon Quests and other activities before scheduling the event.

4
Underestimating Enemy Mutations

Higher difficulties bring mutated enemies with unique abilities that many players ignore, focusing only on standard zombie waves.

Fix: Learn what each mutation does and prepare your defense accordingly.

5
Not Reinforcing Alliance Members

Struggling allies fall faster without support, which caps how many waves the whole alliance can clear.

Fix: Send reinforcements to weaker members — more alliance waves means better rewards for everyone.

6
Not Having Enough Troops

A half-empty squad leaves gaps in your wall's defense.

Fix: Make sure you have enough healthy troops to fill your squads — a full squad makes a huge difference.

7
Logging Off Without Setting Your Defense

You can still earn rewards while offline, but only if your defense is actually ready.

Fix: Station your best heroes and troops before you log off.

Undead Siege rewards teamwork more than individual strength. Prepare your wall, station heroes, reinforce teammates, keep your squads full, and coordinate with your alliance. Avoiding these 7 mistakes will help your alliance reach higher waves and unlock better milestone rewards.
