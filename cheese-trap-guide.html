Alliance boss event guide

Cheese Trap

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The event

A cooperative alliance boss event where everyone rallies together to attack Cheese Thieves for rewards.

1. Preparation phase

Donate Mouse Lure to the Cheese Trap. Donations provide Alliance Contribution Points and Damage Buffs for attacking Cheese Thieves.

＋Mouse Lure comes from Daily Quest chest rewards — you probably already have some. Donate 1–2 per Cheese Trap.
＋Once the alliance hits the max donation requirement, extra donations still earn Contribution Points, just no additional buffs.
2. Extermination phase
＋R4/R5 members manually activate the Cheese Trap and start the rallies.
＋Alliance members attack through those rallies to deal damage.
＋Each Cheese Trap stays active for 30 minutes.

Only R4/R5-led rallies count —
maximum participation wins the phase.

Strategy

R4/R5 create rallies for members to join and deliver damage. Only R4/R5 rallies receive damage buffs, so join a led rally instead of starting your own.
