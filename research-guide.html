The Core Idea

Growing fast isn't about researching everything — it's about researching the right things.

Spreading research across every tree equally feels productive, but it actually slows your account down.

Research Priority Order
1

Development — build and research faster.

2

Economy — keep your resources flowing so progress never stalls.

3

Hero — strengthen the commanders leading every battle.

4

Soldier — improve your troop performance for PvE and PvP.

5

Everything else — unlock and invest as your account grows and your priorities change.

Don't chase power. Build momentum.

The best research isn't always the one that gives the biggest power increase — it's the one that makes every future upgrade faster, cheaper, or more efficient. That's how strong kingdoms are built.

Play smart. Strike strategic.
