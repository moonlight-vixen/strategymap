Don't Get Zero'd

No shield = no safety.

Without a shield, enemy players can attack your city while you're away.

Shield When
Logging Off
Sleeping
Busy / Not Checking
What Happens Without a Shield
Troops Gone
Resources Gone
Progress Lost
Important Notes
＋Shields do not protect troops outside your city — keep troops home before logging off.
＋Keep backup shields in your bag:
24-Hour Shield x1–2, 8-Hour Shield x1–2.
These can be found in the Nomad Trader Shop.
＋Re-shield before it expires — don't wait until the timer runs out.

Shield > Rebuild. Always.
A shield costs less than replacing lost troops and resources.
