Death Prison event guide

Falcon Quests Guide

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The Threat

A death row prison shrouded in thick fog has been ravaged by plague. The inmates and guards within have been infected, transforming into savage Blights. Hordes of Blights now threaten to assault us.

Complete Falcon Quests to gather clues, pinpoint the prison's location, and prepare our defenses.

Event Activation — Falcon Quests

Alliance members acquire clues by completing Falcon Quests:

Defeat Rebels+300
Vanquish Blight Legion+300
Slay Wandering Blight+300
Clear Blights+10
Aid Alliance Members+10
Resource Recovery+10
Harvest Resources+10
Alliance Excavation+10
＋Once enough clues are gathered, R4/R5 members can dispatch Falcons to locate the challenge.
＋The prison is available in multiple difficulty levels — higher difficulties mean stronger enemies but greater rewards.
＋Before starting the search, R4/R5 members may manually select the difficulty based on the alliance's strength.
＋After each successful event activation, the search enters cooldown.
Requirements
1

Your territory must meet the minimum level requirement for the selected difficulty.

2

Being in the alliance for more than 48 hours; offline time under 72 hours.

3

Territory is not currently in a cross-server state.

Event Rules
1

Once the event officially begins, the prison unleashes waves of Blights attacking eligible members' territories.

2

Blight attacks ignore shield protection — make sure your troops are positioned for defense.

3

Once a member's territory is breached, they won't be targeted by Blights for the remainder of the event.

Reward Details
＋The more waves members successfully defend, the higher their personal rewards.
＋The more waves the alliance collectively defends, the higher the alliance rewards.

Alliance rewards are only claimable by members who have been in the alliance for over 48 hours.
