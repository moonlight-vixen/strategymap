Alliance vs. alliance event guide

Elixir Scramble

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

The event

A 30-minute Alliance vs. Alliance event. Capture buildings, gather Elixir, control objectives, and earn the most points to win.

Event times

Times vary by team — check Special Events → Elixir Scramble to see your local time and assigned team.

Timeline
1

Register — R4/R5 build the teams.

2

5-minute prep — leadership assigns objectives.

3

30-minute battle — buildings unlock in phases.

Objective
＋Capture and hold buildings.
＋Gather Elixir.
＋Defeat enemy players.
＋Control high-value objectives.
Battle phases
Phase 1 · 0–10 min
＋Capture the first 4 buildings.
＋Build an early points lead.
Phase 2 · 10+ min
＋Contest the Elixir Castle and other high-value buildings.
Late Game
＋Hold buildings, reinforce teammates, and maintain map control.
Strategy
＋Prioritize objectives over random PvP.
＋Control Healing Camps to keep your troops healed and deny the enemy free healing.
＋Defend and retake buildings.
＋Teamwork wins battles.

Signup process
1

Registration — tap Select Participants and Request to Battle at a time that works for you. If you don't register, R4/R5 can't add you to a team.

2

Team selection — R4/R5 build teams from registered players. Main Team enters at the start; substitutes join after the battle begins if needed.

3

Matchmaking — after registration closes, alliances are matched against opponents of similar strength (based on the selected team's total power).

4

Preparation — Main Team enters the battlefield. Leadership assigns buildings, roles, and objectives.

5

Battle — capture buildings, hold them, and defeat enemies to outscore the opposing alliance.

Register before the deadline, be online if selected, and follow leadership assignments.
