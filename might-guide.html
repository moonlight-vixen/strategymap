Where power comes from
Levels

Small, steady gains over time.

Stars

Huge power spikes — the real power.

Levels give small, steady gains. Stars unlock huge power spikes. Don't judge a hero until they reach their breakpoints — same hero, different monster.

The beginner trap
✕Quitting on a hero too early
✕Spreading resources across too many heroes
✕Judging power only by early-game feel
✕Low stars, weak gear, and missing passives — then blaming the hero

It's not the hero — it's the investment. Patience = Power.

Tactical takeaways
Skills scale from your stats, not fixed numbers.
Stars unlock massive power jumps.
Same hero, different investment = different results.
Scaling heroes need patience — stars matter more than you think.

Read smart. Invest wise. Dominate.
