Raiding & War Tactics

APEX · Last Asylum Alliance Strategy & Reference Hub

Before You Raid
＋Scout before you strike.
＋Target weakness — farms, mills, and weak cities.
＋Use rallies to hit harder.
＋Coordinate with your alliance.
During the Raid
＋Attack when shields are down.
＋Take what you need, leave the rest.
＋Keep troops safe — don't get greedy.
＋Rotate targets to avoid counterattacks.
War Philosophy
＋Intel is power — scout constantly.
＋Speed is life — move faster, decide faster.
＋Supply lines win wars — cut where it hurts.
＋Defense is offense — a strong defense lets you attack with confidence.
＋Patience pays — wait for the right moment, then strike hard.

Raid smart. Take more. Lose less.

Scout hard. Strike smart. Win the exchange.
